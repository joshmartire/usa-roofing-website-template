Roofing in Brentwood, TN

Brentwood's homes tend to be larger, with steep, cut-up roofs, plenty of skylights and lots surrounded by mature hardwoods. Those roofs take more care to replace, and the trees put extra wear on shingles, valleys and gutters.

Free Roof Quote Instant estimate

Brentwood homeowners call us most often for replacements on homes from the 1970s through the 1990s, storm repairs after wind and hail, and premium materials that suit a larger home. Big roofs with many valleys, dormers and skylights need careful flashing and planning to last.

Because lots and exposure vary so much across the city, we match materials, flashing and ventilation to your specific home instead of using one standard everywhere. Below you'll find each of our services with information specific to Brentwood homes.

Permits in Brentwood

Building permits in Brentwood are handled by the City of Brentwood. Replacing a roof with similar materials often doesn't require a permit, but structural changes, new roof framing and new skylight openings typically do, and many neighborhoods also have HOA design rules. We confirm requirements for your property before work starts.
