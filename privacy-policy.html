Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your personal information.

Effective date: October 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Hammerline Roofing ("we", "us") collects, uses and protects personal information through this website and when you contact us about your roof. We handle personal information in accordance with applicable United States and state privacy laws.

Information we collect

Information you give us. When you submit our quote form or send us an instant estimate, we collect your name, phone number, email address, property address or area, the service you're interested in and any details you choose to share. Estimate submissions also include the roof measurements, roof details you selected and the estimated price range shown to you. If you call or email us, we collect the information you provide in that conversation.

Instant roof estimate. If you use our instant roof estimate, the address you type is sent to an address-search service (Photon by komoot, with OpenStreetMap Nominatim as a backup) to find its location. That location is then used to request satellite imagery (Esri World Imagery) and building outlines (OpenStreetMap data from OpenFreeMap, with the Overpass API as a backup). These services receive the address or map location and your IP address, as any website does when your browser requests content, and handle it under their own privacy policies. The address, measurements and estimate are not sent to us unless you choose to submit the estimate form.

Technical information. When you visit, our hosting provider's servers may automatically log technical information such as your IP address and browser details for security and operational purposes. We do not use this website to collect analytics, and we do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your inquiry and arrange a roof inspection.
  • To prepare quotes, contracts and work schedules.
  • To communicate with you about your project before, during and after the work.
  • To meet legal, accounting, tax and warranty obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for unrelated marketing without your consent.

Who we share it with

Quote and estimate requests submitted through this website are transmitted through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a third-party form service that delivers submissions to our email. Your information may also be shared with suppliers or subcontractors when it's needed to deliver your project, and with professional advisors or authorities where required by law. Service providers we use may store or process information outside your state or outside the United States, where it may be subject to the laws of those jurisdictions.

How long we keep it

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above, including any period required for legal, tax or warranty reasons, and then securely delete or destroy it.

How we protect it

We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please don't include sensitive information, such as financial details, in our website forms.

Your choices and rights

You may ask to access the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or withdraw your consent to our use of it, subject to legal and contractual restrictions. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

Cookies

This website does not set cookies. See our Cookie Policy for details on the limited browser storage the site uses.

Contact us

Questions about privacy can be sent to info@example.com or by phone at (615) 555-0147.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above shows when it was last changed.
