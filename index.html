Anatomy of a roof

Five layers.
Zero shortcuts.

  1. 01Solid deck

    Old roofing comes off and every sheet of sheathing is checked. Soft or rotted wood is replaced so the new roof has something solid to hold onto.

  2. 02Ice & water shield

    Self-adhering membrane at the eaves and in the valleys, where wind-driven rain and heavy runoff try to force water under the shingles.

  3. 03Underlayment & drip edge

    Synthetic underlayment over the rest of the deck, with metal drip edge so water drops into the gutter instead of behind it.

  4. 04Shingles & flashing

    Starter strips, architectural shingles nailed to the manufacturer's pattern, and new flashing at every wall, chimney and pipe.

  5. 05Ridge vent & cap

    A continuous ridge vent lets the attic breathe, finished with ridge cap shingles that lock the top of the roof down.

All FAQs

How accurate is the instant roof estimate?

It's a ballpark for a standard asphalt shingle reshingle, based on your roof's outline in satellite imagery and the pitch you choose. Hidden deck damage, extra layers, chimneys, skylights and complex roof shapes can change the final price, which is confirmed in writing after an on-site inspection.

How do I know if I need a new roof or just a repair?

Isolated damage can usually be repaired. Widespread curling, cracking, granule loss or repeated leaks usually mean the roof is near the end of its life. After an inspection, we'll tell you which applies and show you photos.

How long does a roof replacement take?

Most homes take one to three days. Size, steepness, the number of old layers and the weather all affect the schedule.

What happens if you find rotten decking?

Damaged sheathing has to be replaced before the new roof goes on. We list a per-sheet price in your quote in advance, show you what we found and only replace what needs it.
